I've spent the last few years building products that scale — from municipal AI
systems serving cities across the U.S. to IoT fulfillment solutions for major
retailers. Along the way I've collected far more lessons than fit in a résumé
bullet. This blog is where I'll write them down.

## What to expect

I'll be posting about the things I actually work on day to day:

- **Architecture decisions** — the trade-offs behind serverless, multi-tenant,
  and cloud-native systems.
- **SvelteKit & TypeScript** — patterns, gotchas, and the occasional deep dive.
- **Product notes** — what shipping real features for real users teaches you.
- **News & updates** — milestones from the projects I'm building.

## Why write at all?

Writing forces clarity. If I can't explain a decision in plain prose, I probably
don't understand it well enough yet. Publishing in the open also means the notes
might help someone else hit the same wall a little less hard.

> The best way to learn something is to explain it to someone else.
